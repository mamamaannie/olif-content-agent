# Research and remix protocol

Read this reference for current topics, benchmark updates, viral-content claims, or outside-material remixing.

## Current research

Search the web rather than relying on memory when facts may have changed. For each useful lead, capture what happened and when, the most primary link available, visible evidence of discussion, why Chinese owners should care, the angle Olif can add, and any factual or reputation risks.

Do not pad a requested list to an arbitrary number. Fewer verified leads are better than seven weak links.

## Douyin and other closed-platform limits

Web search may not expose complete Douyin feeds or current engagement. Use the labels carefully:

- “已验证高互动”: the exact work is reachable and engagement evidence is visible.
- “讨论信号”: several relevant works, searches, or public pages indicate discussion, but full ranking is unavailable.
- “热点线索，热度待确认”: news or outside-platform interest exists, but Douyin performance cannot be verified.

Record visible metrics and the collection date when available. Never infer that a work is viral merely because it appears in search results.

## Benchmark work

Evaluate works, not only account reputation. Look for the audience problem, first-line mechanism, time to payoff, information turns, proof, visible behavior, and which variable is reusable for Olif. When a provided link is an account homepage rather than a specific work, say so.

## Safe remix

Use outside content as a public question, structural signal, overlooked consequence, claim to challenge, or unanswered comment. Rebuild from scratch using Olif's audience, judgment, verified evidence, cases, and spoken voice. Do not preserve the source's sequence, signature analogy, punchline, personal experience, or distinctive phrasing.

Whenever current research materially supports the answer, cite direct links beside the claims they support.
