# OLIF creator profile

## Identity and positioning

- Name on camera: 杜俣桐 Olif.
- Roles: 中英青年协会会长；杜克船长出海数字科技中心创始人。
- Primary platform: 抖音.
- Slogan: “帮助中国老板在全球赚钱”. Treat this as positioning, never as an earnings guarantee.
- Account role: a young-looking, non-stuffy operator who has worked on real overseas projects and can translate complex decisions into language owners understand.
- Natural voice: relaxed, slightly funny, candid, and rich in quotable lines. Avoid bureaucratic consulting language and forced comedy.

## Audience and outcome

Primary viewers are Chinese owners of consumer and industrial businesses who have not started going overseas, are testing a first market, or have spent an initial budget and worry they chose the wrong market or method.

Content goals can differ by post:

- Traffic: expand reach and followers through business conflict, overseas observations, cross-cultural contrast, people, opportunity, and current events.
- Trust: demonstrate market judgment, strategy, resources, and execution through cases, frameworks, and failure analysis.
- Conversion: attract qualified owners and filter out mismatched expectations or budgets.

Do not make every post convert. Maintain an Olif lens even in broad-traffic content: global markets, owners' decisions, cross-cultural reality, local resources, or commercial strategy.

## Business and competitive advantage

Olif handles strategy, resource matching, and subsequent execution and takes responsibility for delivery. Strength is not a single service menu; it is the ability to combine and mobilize global resources into a workable market-entry path.

- Strong regions: Europe, Middle East, and Africa.
- Has directly cooperative resources across most mainstream markets.
- Relevant modules: overseas social-media operations, influencer marketing, media relations and releases, websites and brand setup, events, association or institutional relationships, business advertising, and offline channels.
- Overseas social-media operations usually require at least several thousand RMB per month; do not turn this into a universal price promise.

## Confirmed case material and boundaries

### Yeast brand in Africa

- Previously relied heavily on offline distribution and held a leading African-market position.
- Faster-growing European competitors began to threaten its share of voice.
- The team proposed social-media growth, worked with 10 creators, and generated more than 10 million impressions.
- Country scope, platforms, duration, engagement quality, and causal sales impact remain unconfirmed. Do not invent them.

### Tire brand: Germany to Africa

- The owner initially chose Germany because its automotive industry is strong.
- Certification consumed more than RMB 100,000 and several months without completing the required qualifications.
- Olif helped re-plan toward Africa, build a website, and operate overseas social media.
- Current monthly sales exceed RMB 1 million and the company plans a Kenya branch.
- Do not claim one marketing action alone caused all revenue.

### Beauty factory and UK brand history

- A factory with declining performance registered a brand in the UK, worked with local influencers, sponsored real events, and held an activity with a Cambridge University laboratory.
- Never say “Cambridge University certified, endorsed, recommended, or validated the product.”
- The later statement that the brand sells hundreds of millions of RMB domestically per year still needs a confirmed measurement basis and publication permission.
- Do not attribute the full commercial result to Olif.

### Story seeds that are not complete cases

- A Tianjin pile-driver factory had struggled to pay salaries and gradually improved after developing overseas business.
- The heir of an African bread business worked as a UK property manager; Olif and he later helped bring brands into Africa.
- Both need factual interviews before a complete story can be written.

## Default fact discipline

- Ask only when a client number, institution relationship, result attribution, brand identity, or private story needs confirmation.
- Protect identifiable client information unless public permission is clear.
- Distinguish what Olif did from what the client, product, channels, or market also contributed.
- Prefer “共同办过活动” over invented institutional endorsement.
