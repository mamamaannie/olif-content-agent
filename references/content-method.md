# Content method and quality bar

This reference distills the useful principles learned from 发疯姥子, benchmark accounts, external samples, and prior failed drafts. It is guidance, not text to imitate.

## Core content equation

Use a topic the audience already cares about, express it in a form they want to hear, and prove it with an experience, case, or judgment only Olif can credibly provide.

> public problem × Olif's clear judgment × distinctive evidence × useful next step

Start from what an owner fears losing—money, time, orders, credibility, market opportunity, or decision quality—not from a service name or whatever case is easiest to tell.

For trust and conversion, use verified experience or evidence. For traffic, a clearly tagged fictional, composite, or dramatized narrative may embody the public problem without becoming a factual proof claim.

## Value before knowledge

Clarify internally: who does this help, in what situation, to make what better decision? “利他” means improving a decision: whether to do it, what to do first, how to allocate a first budget, what to avoid, how to choose a partner or market, and what outcome counts as real. It does not mean unloading terminology.

## Opening and progression

- In the first three seconds, make the right viewer recognize the personal stake through loss, payoff, conflict, result, or a defensible counterintuitive judgment.
- Start paying off the promise within ten seconds.
- Add meaningful information, a turn, or a concrete example approximately every 12–15 seconds.
- Use one central claim per piece. Cases prove the claim; they should not hijack the topic.
- Generate multiple hooks internally if useful, but normally show the user only the strongest one.
- A factual hook must remain true. A traffic-led fictional or composite hook may be constructed for retention when the creative mode is disclosed to the user backstage.

## Traffic-led fictionalization

- When account heat is the priority, do not refuse or weaken a strong script merely because the matching first-person experience did not literally happen.
- The skill may create or combine personal experience, anonymous characters, dialogue, pressure, reversals, and scene details to improve identification and propagation.
- Deliver the strongest script normally, then add one compact backstage label: `创作性虚构`, `合成改编`, or `戏剧化处理`. Do not automatically insert a disclaimer into the spoken copy.
- The user retains the final decision about whether and how to publish the creative material.
- Do not use creative mode to fabricate checkable third-party claims: identifiable-person allegations, client performance, exact figures, institutional endorsement or certification, cooperation relationships, or commercial causality. Keep them verified, anonymous and hypothetical, or remove them.

## Voice and trust

- Write in meaning blocks and short spoken sentences, not essay paragraphs.
- Let humor come from comparison, mild mockery, or reality contrast. Do not make high-value services feel unserious.
- Avoid empty words such as “赋能、布局、生态、闭环、助力” unless the sentence contains a concrete action and consequence.
- Do not open every post with credentials. State the owner's problem, give the judgment, then use experience as proof.
- A line is valuable when it sounds like something Olif would naturally say and another ordinary agency could not credibly repeat unchanged.

## Content jobs and lengths

- Traffic: shareable conflict, people, overseas reality, opportunity, current events. No required CTA.
- Trust: cases, market decisions, execution details, failure review, longer tutorials. Usually no hard CTA.
- Conversion: expectation-setting, service fit, budget readiness, common buying mistakes. One clear next action is enough.

Test different lengths rather than forcing uniformity: 15–35 seconds for one conflict and judgment; 45–90 seconds for one explained problem; 2–8 minutes for full stories, case reviews, or tutorials.

## Benchmark lessons

- 司拜科-style strength: a complete, useful outcome and clear promise. Adapt information completeness, not lecture length.
- 大圣外贸思考-style strength: sharp commercial judgment in daily language. Avoid overconfident binary predictions.
- 程前-style strength: people, pressure, turning points, and consequences. Keep a factual case accurate; when stronger dramatization is needed, switch to a backstage-labeled fictional or composite mode instead of silently altering a named real case.
- 发疯姥子-style strength: pain-first topic research, useful pacing, personal evidence, comment-driven iteration, and low-cost publishing loops. Do not reuse recognizable wording.

## Selection and quality gate

Reject or rebuild when the title is merely a service/country/category; the opening does not say why an owner should care; the body cannot pay off the hook quickly; any agency could say it unchanged; an unverified number or causal claim is presented as factual proof; or the piece fails its traffic/trust/conversion job.

Before recommending a mature script, check audience stake, first-three-second relevance, payoff within ten seconds, concrete value, Olif-specific evidence, natural spoken voice, and factual safety. Prefer one strong recommendation over a menu that makes the user do the editorial judgment.
